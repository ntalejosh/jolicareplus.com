ABOUT JOLI

A family-run service, built around home.

JOLI Care Plus LLC is a family-run, private-pay, non-medical home support service in Massachusetts.

Support starts with listening: the routines, pressure points, and what matters most at home. Support is agreed together and discussed clearly before care begins, and the conversation stays open as needs change.
