Who We Are

A Trusted Partner in Home Health Care

JOLI Care Plus is a professional licensed in-home service agency in Massachusetts. We care for Alzheimer's and dementia patients, the geriatric population, individuals with developmental disabilities, and those facing health challenges.

Our dedicated team serves clients in private homes, senior communities, assisted living facilities, group homes, and nursing homes — improving lives through compassionate care.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide personalized and empathetic care with respect and dignity, ensuring our clients remain safe and independent at their place of residence.

🔭

Our Vision

To become the leading home health care agency in Massachusetts, known for quality professional services — helping clients live in the safest, healthiest, and happiest state of being.

Companionship

Mobility Assistance

🩺

Health Monitoring

Regular checking of vitals with detailed reporting so you always know how they're doing.

💊

Medication Support

Household Support

🚗

Transportation

Safe accompaniment to doctor visits, pharmacy trips, and outings to stay connected.

📋

Care Documentation

Thorough recording of health changes and nutrition monitoring shared with your family.
