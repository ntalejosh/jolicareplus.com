Who We Are

A Trusted Partner in Home Health Care

JOLI Care Plus is a professional licensed in-home service agency in Massachusetts. We care for Alzheimer's and dementia patients, the geriatric population, individuals with developmental disabilities, and those facing health challenges.

Our dedicated team serves clients in private homes, senior communities, assisted living facilities, group homes, and nursing homes — improving lives through compassionate care.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide personalized and empathetic care with respect and dignity, ensuring our clients remain safe and independent at their place of residence.

🔭

Our Vision

To become the leading home health care agency in Massachusetts, known for quality professional services — helping clients live in the safest, healthiest, and happiest state of being.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Care Services

A full spectrum of in-home support tailored to each individual's unique needs and goals.

🛁

Personal Care

Bathing, showering, grooming, dressing, and skincare — delivered with dignity and respect for each client's comfort.

💛

Companionship

Meaningful social interaction, conversation, and emotional support to combat loneliness and enhance quality of life.

🚶

Mobility Assistance

Safe transfers, repositioning, walking support, and fall prevention to maintain independence and confidence.

📋

Health Monitoring

Regular checking of pulse, temperature, and respiration with detailed reporting of any changes to supervisors.

💊

Medication Support

Timely reminders and assistance with self-administration, keeping clients on track with prescribed treatments.

🏡

Household Support

Light housekeeping, laundry, grocery shopping, and nutritious meal preparation for a comfortable home.

🚗

Transportation

Safe accompaniment to medical appointments, pharmacy visits, and social outings to stay connected.

📝

Documentation

Thorough recording of condition changes, vital signs tracking, and nutrition monitoring shared with care teams.
