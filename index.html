CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Know what JOLI is - and what it is not.

Clear scope protects the person receiving care, the family, and the caregiver. JOLI currently provides private-pay, non-medical support.

JOLI can support

  • Personal-care routines
  • Companionship and activities
  • Meal preparation
  • Light housekeeping and laundry
  • Transportation and accompaniment

JOLI does not provide

  • Nursing or clinical assessment
  • Diagnosis or medical advice
  • Medication administration
  • Emergency response
  • Services outside a caregiver's agreed role
